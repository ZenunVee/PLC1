How TPE Works

Our advanced Therapeutic Plasma Exchange process safely removes harmful substances from your blood while preserving essential components, promoting optimal health and longevity.

Step-by-Step TPE Process:

1
Blood Separation

Blood is drawn through a small catheter and circulated through an FDA-approved apheresis machine.

2
Plasma Removal

The machine separates plasma from the blood cells. The plasma, which may contain damaging antibodies or inflammatory proteins, is removed.

3
Replacement

The blood cells are combined with donor plasma or a safe replacement fluid and returned to your body.

4
Restoration

By reducing the harmful triggers in your blood, your immune and nervous systems can function more effectively, allowing natural healing and symptom improvement.

Most sessions last about 2-3 hours, and many patients rest, read, or listen to music during treatment.
